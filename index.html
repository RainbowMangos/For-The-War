§3 World structure

Three layers, one tile

Movement follows explicit connections, never the decorative roads. Each layer reads game state; only the strategic and development layers are authoritative.

Strategic

authoritative

Tile ownership, city centres, connections, national borders, unit positions and objectives. Ground units move only along road, bridge, sea, river and dock links; capture happens at a tile centre.

Development

authoritative

Zones and tiers per tile, production sites, infrastructure and tile health. City centres are never zoned. Each city owns a fixed domain split into regions of about 10 tiles.

Environment

view only

Terrain relief, megacities, weather, sun shafts, ground mist, smoke and damage. It must communicate state and never block movement, combat or sync.

§6 Movement

§9 Art direction

Gloom you can read

A cinematic, overcast world at Dreadnought Tartarus scale, with clear information layered on top. Information first, atmosphere second.

What the camera shows

260 m

Thresholds are the live values from the 3D view. The default play distance is 260 m, and the camera ranges from 25 to 2,200 m.

Palette in use

Visual principles

Dark and gloomyMuted colour, charcoal haze, a low amber sun and overcast skies. Saturation sits at 0.78.
Readable at strategic scaleUnits, ownership, routes and city centres stay legible at every zoom. Flags shrink only to 80% far out.
Scale through small thingsSlim roads and small props make cities and land read as vast. Towers rise to 210 m over 30 m tiles.
Atmosphere without clutterClouds, shafts and mist fade by camera distance so they never hide the map.
Persistent decayRuins, scorch and craters now. Next: damage driven by tile health.
